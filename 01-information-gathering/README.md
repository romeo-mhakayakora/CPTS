## 🎯 Domain Goal

Be able to systematically answer:

```text
What exists?
      ↓
What is exposed?
      ↓
What technologies are running?
      ↓
What versions are running?
      ↓
What vulnerabilities / attack surfaces exist?
      ↓
What should I attack first?
```
